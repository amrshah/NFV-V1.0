Do not fill unknowns with guesses. Mark unknown and define the cheapest way to resolve it.

## 3. Mandatory gates before scoring
- Customer problem/outcome is sufficiently clear for the proposed stage.
- Security, privacy, legal/license and tenant/data boundaries are acceptable.
- A credible implementation/deployment path exists.
- Capacity and dependencies are realistic.
- Support and maintenance burden are acknowledged.

Failed gates mean remediate, test, defer or reject. Weighted scores do not override gates.

## 4. Compare unlike investments fairly

Classify each candidate as one or more of:
- **Commitment:** existing customer promise, production reliability, deadline.
- **Revenue bet:** plausible near-term paying customer or repeatable offer.
- **Reusable capability:** reduces cost/time or enables multiple products, with actual consumers identified.
- **Risk reduction:** prevents likely material loss or improves recovery/security.
- **Option/experiment:** buys information or preserves future choices at a bounded cost.

State the evidence standard appropriate to its class. A revenue bet needs customer/distribution evidence; a shared platform needs named consumers and measured integration leverage; an option needs a clear uncertainty and stop condition.
